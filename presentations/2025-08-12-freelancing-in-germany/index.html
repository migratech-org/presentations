LEGAL FOUNDATIONS: Choosing Your Business Structure

Company Types

  • Einzelunternehmen (sole proprietorship – default for freelancers).
  • Partnerships: GbR (civil law partnership), **OHG** (general partnership).
  • Limited Liability Corperations: UG (mini-GmbH), GmbH (limited liability company), AG (Public Limited Company/Aktiengesellschaft).
  • Associations: e.V. (Verein)
  • Cooperative: eG (eingetragene Genossenschaft)
Gewerbe vs. Freiberufler
  • Freiberufler (liberal professions: doctors, lawyers, consultants, artists) → **No Gewerbeanmeldung**, simpler rules
  • Gewerbetreibende (trades, IT, tradespeople) → **Requires Gewerbeanmeldung**, subject to trade tax (Gewerbesteuer)
Special Cases
  • _Kleingewerbe_ regulation (small trade exemption: <€24,500 profit/year).
  • _Kleinunternehmer_ regulation to be exempted from VAT (€25,000 revenue for 1st year and €100,000 for 2nd year)
  • _Cooperatives_ (e.g., **Smart** model for freelancers).
  • _Complex Structures_: UG + Holding (advanced tax optimization).

Most freelancers start as **Freiberufler (Einzelunternehmen)**. Only switch to _Gewerbe_ if your profession qualifies > as a trade.

### **II. SETUP & REGISTRATION: Getting Legally Operational**

- **Institutions & Registrations**: - _Gewerbeamt_: **Gewerbeanmeldung** (trade registration for _Gewerbetreibende_). - _Finanzamt_ (Tax Office): **Tax registration** (→ Tax ID, VAT ID, tax number). - _Handelsregister_: **Mandatory for GmbH/UG** (not for _Freiberufler_ or sole traders). - _IHK_ (Chamber of Commerce): **Voluntary membership** for _Gewerbetreibende_ (useful for networking). - _BZSt_ (Federal Central Tax Office): **VAT ID (USt-IdNr.)** for EU sales. - **Requirements**: - _Work contract_ check in case if you are also working as employee. - Business name, **Business bank account** (not "P-Account" – _P-Konto_ is for personal insolvency). - Address options: **Home address**, **virtual address**, or **coworking space address** (must be _actual_ operational address). - **Registration documents**: Passport, proof of address, business purpose description. - **Registrations**: - _Register on ELSTER_ it takes time to get your account active so register now. - _Legal Structure_ select which structure suits you - _Submit Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung_ to register with the Finanzamt - _Submit Gewerbeanmeldung with your Gewerbeamt_ - _Open Business Bank account_ - _IHK Registration_ Happens automatically like the TV Tax - _Entry in the Handelsregister_ Only for commerical legal forms like: UG, GmbH, AG, OHG, ...etc (Not Einzelunternehmen) - **Foreign Freelancers**: - _Ausländerbehörde_ (Foreigners' Office): **Visa/residence permit** (e.g., Freelancer Visa, EU Blue Card).

### **III. TAX COMPLIANCE: Declarations, Prepayments & Deadlines**

- **Tax Types & Calculations**: - _Income Tax_ (**Einkommensteuer**): On profit (not revenue!). - _VAT_ (**Umsatzsteuer**): Standard 19% (reduced 7% for some services). **VAT ID required for EU sales**. - _Trade Tax_ (**Gewerbesteuer**): Only for _Gewerbetreibende_ (not Freiberufler). - _Corporate Tax_: Only for GmbH/UG. - _Capital Gains Tax_: **Teileinkünfteverfahren** (60% taxed at personal rate). - **Tax Methods**: - **EÜR** (Cash-based accounting): Default for small freelancers (<€800k revenue or <€80k profits). - **Bilanzbuchführung** (Accrual accounting): Required for larger businesses (e.g., UG/GmbH). - **Key Processes**: - **Vorauszahlung**: Monthly/quarterly income tax & VAT prepayments. - **Steuererklärung**: Annual tax return (deadline: July 31). - **Vorsteuer**: Input VAT reclaim (via ELSTER). - **Loss Management**: Losses carry-forward to offset future profits.

### **IV. ACCOUNTING & BOOKKEEPING: Tracking Finances**

_(How to record transactions correctly)_ - **Accounting Rules**: - **Double-entry bookkeeping requirements** (**Buchführungspflicht**): For commercials - **Cash bookkeeping** (_Kassenbuch_): For Freiberufler - **SKR03/SKR04**: Standard accounting charts (SKR03 for trades, SKR04 for freelancers). - **Practical Tools**: - **Invoices**: Must include VAT ID, tax number, legal name/address. - **E-Invoices**: Legally valid if meeting EU standards. Used between B2B and with the Government. - **Software**: Basic Excel or use software like LexOffice/SevDesk - **Key Concepts**: - **Income vs. Profit**: Tax paid on _profit_ (revenue − expenses). - **Depreciation**: **AfA-Tabelle** (asset depreciation schedules). - **Profit-reduction ideas**: Deductible expenses (home office, software, travel).

### **V. SOCIAL SECURITY & INSURANCE**

- **Mandatory Insurances**: - **Health Insurance** (_Krankenversicherung_): Public (e.g., TK) or private. - **Long-term Care Insurance** (_Pflegeversicherung_): Tied to health insurance. - **Pension Insurance** (_Rentenversicherung_): Voluntary for freelancers (not recommended). - **Special Cases**: - **Hybrid Mode**: Freelancer + Employee. Whichever has more than 65% of the income is considered main employment. - **Minijob regulations**: (<€520/month), no social contributions are needed.

### **VI. OPERATIONAL ESSENTIALS: Day-to-Day Running**

_(Beyond taxes – legal business operations)_ - **Website/Legal Requirements**: - **Impressum** (legal notice), **AGB** (terms), **GDPR compliance**, **Privacy notice**, **Copyright notices**. - **EU-Specific Rules**: - **OSS (One-Stop-Shop)**: For EU VAT via **BOP/ZM portal** (simplifies cross-border sales). - **Tax Entry Tools**: - **ELSTER**: Official portal for tax filings (income, VAT, trade tax). - **ELStAM**: Digital tax card for employees (irrelevant for pure freelancers).

### **VII. SPECIAL SCENARIOS & ADVANCED TOPICS**

_(For complex situations)_ - **Foreign Freelancers**: Visa , tax treaties, non-EU VAT rules. - **Multiple Businesses**: Separate **tax numbers** for each entity (e.g., Freiberufler + UG). - **Tax Regulations**: - **GwG** (_Geringwertige Wirtschaftsgüter_): Low-value asset depreciation. - **10-Day Rule**: Using Accural method for purchases around 10-days at the end of the year for recuring invoices. - **Computer Devices**: Full deduction in the year of purchase. - **Hybrid Work**: Navigating **65% rule** to avoid employee reclassification.

### **VIII. COMMON PITFALLS & PRO TIPS**

_(What guides rarely mention)_ - **Address Matters**: Using a virtual address? Must be **physical & verifiable** (no P.O. boxes). - **Fictious self-employment**: If you income is predominatly coming from one client. - **Hire a Steuerberater**: In case you don't understand accounting and legal requirements to avoid any potential fines.
