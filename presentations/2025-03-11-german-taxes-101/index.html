Social Contributions

As an employee, you pay:

  • Health Insurance (Krankenversicherung)
  • Long-term Care Insurance (Pflegeversicherung)
  • Unemployment Insurance (Arbeitslosenversicherung)
  • Pensions (Rentenversicherung)

Key Terminologies: Brutto vs Netto

Brutto (Gross)

  1. Company is paying you money
  2. Social Contributions takes their share from you
  3. Mr. Taxman comes and takes their share from you

Netto (Net)

  4. Whatever is left, is what you get into your bank

Key Terminologies: Taxable Income

It's your:
   Gross Total Global Income
- Social Contributions
- Work-related expenses
- Tax Allowances
= Taxable Income

Key Terminologies: Tax Brackets

  • It's called Steuertarifstufen
  • It's regulated under (EStG § 32a)
  • Progressive Tax System
    • The more you earn, the more you pay in taxes.

Key Terminologies: Tax Brackets

  • Tax amount are divided into 5 zones
    • €12.096: 0%
    • €12.097 to €17.443: 14-24%
    • €17.444 to €68.480: 24-42%
    • €68.481 to €277.825: 42%
    • €277.826 and above: 45%

Key Terminologies: Tax Brackets

Key Terminologies: Solidarity Surcharge

  • Solidarity Surcharge (Solidaritätszuschlag) is regulated under SolzG 1995 and InflAusG Artikel 4
  • It's a 5.5% tax on your income tax.
  • It's applied on high threshold of income tax
    • €19.950 as of 2025
  • It's also applied to other types of taxes
    • For example, Corporate and Capital Gains tax
  • Originally was introduced to fund the Second Gulf War and also the reunification of East and West Germany

Key Terminologies: Tax classes

It's called Steuerklassen.
It's regulated under (EStG § 38b).

  • Class I: Single, divorced, separated, widowed
  • Class II: Single parent
  • Class III: Married, with higher income
  • Class IV: Married, with similar income
  • Class V: Married, with lower income
  • Class VI: Second Employment wages (not freelance), no Tax number provided

Key Terminologies: Social Contributions

  • It's called Sozialabgaben.
  • Mandatory payment to the social security system.
  • They are calculated on the gross taxable income.
  • They aren't affected by work-related expenses.

Key Terminologies: Social Contributions

They are:

  • 14.6% : Gesetzliche Krankenversicherung - SGB V § 241
    • Statutory Health Insurance
  • 1.84% - 4.4% : GKV Zusatzbeitrag - SGB V § 241
    • Additional Health Insurance
    • Percentage is variable
  • 3.4% - 4% : Pflegeversicherung - SGB XI § 55
    • Long-term Care Insurance
    • Percentage depends on having children
  • 18.6% : Rentenversicherung - SGB VI § 287
    • Pension Insurance
  • 2.6% : Arbeitslosenversicherung - SGB III § 341
    • Unemployment Insurance (ALG 1)

Key Terminologies: Tax declaration

It's used to adjust the amount of taxes you have paid and get overpaid taxes refunded.

  • Immigration/Relocation
  • Work-related expenses.
  • Social changes.
  • Medical expenses
  • Work changes
  • New law with tax allowance
  • ...and many many more reasons.

It's always 100% worth it to file a tax declaration!

Key Terminologies: Tax declaration

  • Social contributions are not refundable!
  • For self-employed/freelancers, it can be refunded.

Understanding your payslip

  • Your right to receive a payslip is regulated under GewO § 108
  • It's called Gehaltsabrechnung or Lohnabrechnung in German.
  • It show details of your income, social contributions, and taxes paid.
  • You will receive it monthly.
  • There is a yearly summary called Lohnsteuerbescheinigung and it's used for tax declaration.

Deductions, Allowances, and Tax Credits

  • Deductions: Expenses that reduces taxable income
    • Business expenses, work-related expenses, donations, and medical costs.
    • Steuerabzüge: Invoices will be required
    • Pauschalen: Flat-rate lump sum without documentation

  • Allowances: specific amounts that are exempted from taxation
    • Basic personal allowance, child allowance, disability allowance and capital gains allowance.
    • Freibeträge

  • Tax Credits: Fixed amount that reduces tax liability itself
    • Child tax credit, single-parent tax credit, education/training tax credit.
    • Steuergutschriften

International Taxes

  • Germany has unlimited tax liability
    • Germany imposes taxes on all your worldwide income

  • Double Taxation Agreement between Germany and your country
    • List of Double Taxation Agreements

  • Germany has an exit tax
    • If you leave Germany, you will have to pay an exit tax on your assets in Germany as if you sold them.
